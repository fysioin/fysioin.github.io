Questions

What happens on the first visit?

The physiotherapist asks about your history, examines the area that is troubling you, and explains what is likely going on. Treatment usually starts the same visit, and you leave with a short set of exercises.

Do I need any equipment at home?

No. Most programmes use a chair, a wall, a towel and floor space. If a band or a small weight helps, you will be told exactly what to get, and nothing more.

Is an online consult as useful as a home visit?

For many problems, yes: assessment, exercise progression, posture and follow-up work well over video. If your problem needs hands-on treatment or a physical test, you will be told so and offered a home visit instead.

How do I book, and how long is a session?

Message on WhatsApp at +91 63635 50722. Session length and fees are confirmed on WhatsApp before your slot is booked, so there are no surprises.

Do you treat children?

Enquiries about paediatric physiotherapy are welcome on WhatsApp, and you will be told honestly whether it is something that can be taken on.

What if I need to reschedule?

Message as early as you can on WhatsApp and the slot will be moved. Late cancellations may be charged, and you will be told the policy when you book.

Is this an emergency service?

No. This site and these services are not for emergencies. For chest pain, sudden weakness, difficulty breathing, a severe injury or any urgent concern, contact your doctor or hospital immediately, or call 108.
